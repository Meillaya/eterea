# Code of Conduct

Eterea uses a concise Contributor Covenant-inspired standard for project
participation.

## Our standard

Be respectful, precise, and collaborative. Good-faith disagreement is welcome;
harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and
sustained disruption are not.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
community support spaces, and release/evidence handoffs connected to Eterea.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive content; ask participants to pause
or reframe; or restrict participation for repeated or severe violations. Reports
should use the same private channel guidance as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when
privacy or safety details are involved.

## Project values

- Protect user privacy and local data.
- Credit work accurately and do not misrepresent evidence.
- Keep the shared worktree safe for other contributors.
- Preserve the project's MIT-licensed community expectations with care.
